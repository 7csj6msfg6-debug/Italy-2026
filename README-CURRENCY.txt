CURRENCY CONVERTER UPDATE

What changed:
- Adds Currency converter under More.
- Converts EUR to USD and USD to EUR.
- Retrieves a live reference rate from Frankfurter.
- Saves the last successful rate for offline use.
- Includes quick amounts and optional 0%, 1%, 2%, or 3% card fee.

Deployment:
- Upload all files from this ZIP to the root of the repository and replace existing versions.
- No tickets folder is included or required.
- Private ticket attachments remain on the iPhone as long as the site URL and Safari website data remain unchanged.
