GOOGLE MAPS PASTE UPDATE

What changed:
- The Add Place form now includes a Paste Google Maps link field.
- Pasting a link automatically saves it to the Maps field.
- Full Google Maps URLs can prefill the place name and trip city when those details are present in the URL.
- Google shortened share links hide their destination until opened by Google, so the app preserves the link but may require the name and city to be entered manually.
- Existing saved places and expenses remain unchanged.

Deployment:
Upload the app files to the repository root and replace the current versions. No ticket files are included.
